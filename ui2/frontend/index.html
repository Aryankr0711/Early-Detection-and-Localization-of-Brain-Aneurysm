02 · Neural Pipeline Architecture
Stage 01
Sparse Search
Coarse 3D nnU-Net scans the full brain volume at reduced resolution. DBSCAN clustering identifies the vessel centroid for adaptive ROI cropping.
3D fullres nnU-Net
Folds: 0, 1, 2
DBSCAN centroid extraction
ROI crop: 140×140×140 mm
~8 seconds
Stage 02
Dense Segmentation
Dual nnU-Net ensemble performs high-resolution inference over the cropped ROI. SkeletonRecall and FocalTversky losses produce complementary segmentations.
Model 2A: SkeletonRecall loss
Model 2B: FocalTversky loss
Ensemble voting (13ch)
BBox refinement
~15 seconds
Stage 03
Classification
Transformer classifier with region-masked pooling generates per-vessel probabilities. 5-fold ensemble with optional TTA for robust predictions.
RegionMaskedPooling3D
nnU-Net pretrained backbone
13 location heads + AP head
5-fold × TTA ensemble
~12 seconds
